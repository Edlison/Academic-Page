I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Florida State University, where I am fortunate to work under the supervision of <Ref href="https://yushundong.github.io">Prof. Yushun Dong</Ref>. Before that, I completed my master’s degree at the University of Michigan – Ann Arbor.

<br/>

My research centers on the safety of AI systems, with a particular focus on <span><strong>agent safety</strong></span> and reliable decision-making and execution in autonomous systems. I also investigate <span><strong>model extraction attacks and defenses</strong></span> to understand and strengthen the security of machine learning systems. Ultimately, my goal is to build secure, robust, and trustworthy machine learning systems that contribute to the broader vision of Responsible AI.
